What We Detect

Three Signals, One Score

AttenMind fuses three behavioral signals into a single engagement score that updates in real time.

👁️

Eye State Detection

A Keras CNN analyzes both eyes, fused with Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) geometry to determine if the student's eyes are open or closed.

🥱

Yawn Detection

A deep learning model classifies mouth ROIs as yawning or normal, fused with Mouth Aspect Ratio (MAR) for high accuracy.

🙂

Head Pose Estimation

A custom ResNet-18 predicts yaw and pitch angles. Combined with facial geometry, it detects when students look away.

👥

Multi-Student Support

Detects all faces in a frame simultaneously. Each student is analyzed individually and scores are averaged into a class metric.

📊

Instant Scoring

Eye (40%), Yawn (30%), and Head (30%) contributions are weighted and fused into a final 0–100 engagement score.

🔒

On-Device Privacy

All processing happens locally. No video is transmitted. Students' data stays in the classroom.

Pipeline

How AttenMind Works

1

Frame Capture

Webcam frame grabbed at ~1 fps via OpenCV for real-time analysis.

2

Face Detection

dlib frontal face detector finds all students and ranks them left-to-right.

3

Landmark Extraction

68-point facial landmarks pinpoint eye, mouth, and nose positions precisely.

4

Model Inference

Eye, Yawn and Head Pose models run in parallel per face, fused with geometry.

About AttenMind

An AI-based student engagement monitoring system built with computer vision to support teachers in real-time classroom management.

Project Overview

AttenMind is a final-year capstone project developed at the University of Hail, College of Computer Science and Engineering. The system addresses the challenge of monitoring student engagement in real-time — a task that is inherently difficult in modern classrooms where individual observation is impractical at scale.

The system processes live video frames and extracts three key behavioral indicators: eye state, yawning, and head orientation. These signals are combined through a weighted fusion formula to produce a discipline score from 0 to 100 for each student, as well as a class-wide average.

The Three AI Models

Eye Model

Eye State Classifier

Keras CNN trained on the MRL Eye Dataset. Classifies each eye as open or closed. Fused with EAR (Eye Aspect Ratio) geometric signal at 60% geometric / 40% model weight.

Yawn Model

Yawn Detector

Deep learning classifier (best_model_final.h5) for mouth ROI. Determines yawn vs. no_yawn. Combined with MAR (Mouth Aspect Ratio) at 55%/45% fusion.

Head Pose

Head Pose Estimator

Custom ResNet-18 backbone (headpose_attenmind.pt) predicting yaw and pitch bins. Combined with nose-eye geometric offset for forward-vs-distracted classification.

Scoring Formula

The final engagement score is a weighted combination of three confidence signals:

Score = round(40 × eye_conf + 30 × yawn_conf + 30 × head_conf)

eye_conf = 0.6 × EAR_ramp + 0.4 × model_eye_open_prob
yawn_conf = 0.55 × MAR_no_yawn + 0.45 × (1 – yawn_prob)
head_conf = 0.7 × pose_conf + 0.3 × geom_head_conf

Focused ≥ 80 · Moderate ≥ 50 · Distracted < 50

Technology Stack

Python TensorFlow / Keras PyTorch OpenCV dlib ResNet-18 Streamlit NumPy 68-point Facial Landmarks EAR / MAR Geometry

Privacy & Ethics

AttenMind is designed with privacy preservation as a core principle. All inference runs on-device — no video, images, or facial data are transmitted to any external server. The system focuses solely on behavioral engagement signals and does not perform facial recognition or store biometric identifiers.
